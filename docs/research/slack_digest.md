## Data facts (confirmed by organisers)

- **Normalisation**: released expression is exactly log(1 + 1e4 * c / sum c); `expm1(X).sum(1) == 10000` per cell
  (2026-10-16). **The scorer never renormalises**: raw counts are scored as if log-normalised, with no warning.
- **Cell limits** are inclusive and enforced at both ends (`min_cells <= n <= max_cells`); veckit does not check them.
- **Coordinates**: per-embryo local, not registered across stages; **units and calibration unconfirmed**; z advances in
  fixed steps per section, so treat z as a section step index, not a distance (median z step 14 at E8.75, 15 at
  E8.25_late and E9.5). No ROI or mask files exist. Raw RMS radii are not comparable across stages: E8.25_late
  (RMS 354) includes yolk sac and non-cardiac cells, while E8.75 (RMS 217) is mostly heart without yolk sac
  (Qingquan Zhang, 2026-09-24). Within-stage embryo size also varies a lot.
- **E8.25_late** = slightly later than E8.25, with a more developed heart tube; exact stage being confirmed.
- **Annotations**: manually curated from early-stage marker genes. E8.25 and E8.75 were jointly annotated with the
  same label definitions; E9.5 was annotated separately (not harmonised). No official cross-stage mapping yet; a
  reference table for `celltype` is "prepared, with a few rows still being confirmed". `cm_celltype` exists only in
  E8.0, E8.25_late, E8.75 and E9.5; it is a second-level heart-lineage annotation where "Unknown" means "no subtype
  given". Same string does not imply same population across stages.
- **SHA-256 checksums** for the nine training files were posted (to go on the Data page). E8.25_late.h5ad is
  225,091,568 bytes, SHA-256 d56f94d3e2d719ed3074d86c03eae457ccf90b24145d33300f9958f0e8078593.
- **T2 E8.5 (heart interpolation target)** is released at the start of the final phase. For interpolation boards,
  copy_last uses the previous stage.
- **E7.75** has never been released and never will be (it is the T2 embryo test target).
- A **Data Protocol** section (units, sectioning per stage, label dictionary) is promised "before the test phase
  opens"; not yet published as of 2026-10-18.

## Task 1 dissection protocol (Google Doc + thread, 2026-08-20)

- T1 data were generated by Qingquan Zhang and Neil Chi (UCSD, 10x Multiome), same group as MERFISH. The Qiu 2024
  reference on the atlas viewer was a metadata error.
- E8.5, E9.5, E10.5: **heart dissected together with surrounding structures** (pericardium/chest wall, dorsal tissue
  adjacent to the heart; neural tube and most yolk sac removed). The younger the embryo, the more non-cardiac tissue.
- **E12.5: "only the heart chamber"**: dissected precisely with most surrounding tissue removed, though a few cells
  from the esophagus-heart junction remain. Contamination is "minimal" at E10.5 and "even less" at E12.5.
- **The scorer scores all cells**, including non-cardiac carry-over, so the target composition reflects the dissection.
- "All the late stage heart tissue come[s] from previous stage heart tissue"; non-cardiac cells may help as context or
  can be removed.
- Barcode suffixes `_1`/`_2` are sample/biological-replicate IDs. Each replicate pools several embryos. Replicates:
  E8.5 2, E9.5 2, E10.5 3, E12.5 4 (E12.5 about 3 hearts). Exact embryo counts were not recorded.
- The cell-type counts "33/22 with 5 shared" on the site were MERFISH numbers; the scRNA files have 18 and 21 with 11
  shared. Organisers suggest building a cross-stage label map yourself (e.g. with an LLM).
