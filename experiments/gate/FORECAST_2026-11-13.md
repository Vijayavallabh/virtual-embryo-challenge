## What it rests on

Almost all of the upper tail comes from three assumed facts:
1. **T3 meets s1's bet on its own knockout run (stand-in A).**
   - Weight 0.5. This is the estimator's own rule, chosen there and never measured.
   - It accounts for every draw at or above 203.
   - Set the weight to 0 and P(>= 203) falls to 0.03%. Set it to 0.25 and P(>= 203) is 10% and P(>= 209) is 1.1%. At
     0.75 they are 29% and 3.2%.
   - This is the largest single judgement in the forecast.
2. **E12.5 is deeper than library 3 (T1).**
   - Weight 4/7 at 1.37 times library 3's depth and 1/7 at 1.0. This is try T30's census prior: each released library
     gets one vote.
   - Depth 1.37 carries 77% of the draws at or above 209.
   - T30 measured the base effect at zero time gap. I scaled it to 0.2 of that size, inside R6's "a tenth to a third".
     That scale is judgement. At 0.1 P(>= 209) is 1.5%, and at 0.33 it is 3.6%.
3. **E7.75 was measured like E8.0 (embryo).**
   - Weight 0.45, the assistant's own "0.4 to 0.5" from decision log 13 Nov (a).
   - This world carries 69% of the draws at or above 209.
   - Between 0.3 and 0.6, P(>= 209) runs from 1.8% to 2.4%.

The old procedure's priors swing the result as much as any new judgement. Over the stated alternative priors, P(>= 209)
runs from 0.5% (the heart's released-truth prior) to 4.2% (the heart's thin family). For T1's grid it runs from 1.0% to
2.6%. The chance that T3's factor carries to beta-catenin (0.6) hardly matters: P(>= 209) stays at 2.1% between 0.35
and 0.85.

The boards are taken as independent, as in the estimator. No committed result links one board's hidden facts to
another's.
