### Method

Counts on the shared genes are renormalised to log1p CP10k (identical to .X on the full panel). Per-gene StandardScaler (clipped at +-10 SD) and 50-PC PCA are both fitted on the reference, then logistic regression (C=1, standardised PCs) or kNN (k=15). classifier='auto' runs stratified 5-fold CV on the reference and keeps the higher macro-F1; LR won every time. Coarse groups are sums of fine-label probabilities under configs/lineage_map.yaml. Unassigned means top probability < 0.5. Reference 'Unknown' labels and labels with < 10 cells are excluded from training. Novelty is max(kNN distance ratio, distance from the reference PCA subspace), each divided by the reference's 99th percentile; it is reported but not used to unassign by default. Grouped splits use z sections from spatial_3D as group ids (no sample ids exist in the WT files): 40 sections at E9.5, 35 at E8.75, 36 in the KO.

### Composition estimators (results/shift_estimators.csv, contrast_estimators.csv)

Slope = estimated vs true change; 1 is unbiased. Synthetic pure prior shift (WT E9.5 hold-out resampled with log-normal sigma=0.5 weights, 200 x 5,000 cells): EM 0.995-0.998 (MAE 0.0019-0.0021), top call 0.847-0.890, mean probability 0.781-0.849. Real composition contrast with known truth (lower vs upper half of an embryo's sections; total variation 0.26-0.50): within E9.5 top call 0.997 (label) / 1.005 (group), mean probability 0.948 / 0.979, EM 1.124 / 1.042 with 2-4x larger delta MAE (0.0086 vs 0.0019 at label level). Within E8.75: top 1.006 / 0.975, mean 0.970 / 0.972, EM 1.152 / 1.070. Cross-stage, transferable cells only: E8.75(thinned)->E9.5 top 0.853, mean 0.821, EM 0.827 (MAE 0.0150 / 0.0161 / 0.0213); E9.5->E8.75 top 0.909, mean 0.883, EM 1.018 (MAE 0.0137 / 0.0142 / 0.0167). Conclusion: top-call counting is the best within-stage estimator. EM over-corrects when cells of one type differ between conditions, which is what real contrasts look like.

### Novelty does not catch missing cell types

On held-out reference cells it is calibrated (1.5-1.7% above 1). In a synthetic test where the novel type's marker genes never vary in the reference, the kNN-only score gave median 1.18; I added the PCA-residual component, which separates 100% of novel cells from 2% of known ones. On real data it does not flag absent types: E8.75 neural tube under the E9.5 model 5.5%, E8.75 ExEM 2.6%, E9.5 EpicardiumST under the E8.75 model 12.5%, KO Neural Tube 5.5%. Transferable groups score 1-15%. Unassigned rates for absent types (10-51%) also overlap those of present ones (2-27%).

### Depth

Median counts per cell are 75 (E9.5), 95 (KO) and 130 (E8.75). Thinning the deeper reference to the query depth helps (+2.0 points group accuracy, +2.3 macro-F1, E8.75->E9.5). Thinning a deeper query with one draw hurts (-1.9 points, E9.5->E8.75). Averaging 5 draws matches no thinning (0.790 vs 0.788) and removes most depth-driven novelty (24.5% -> 5.9%). The predict default was changed from 1 to 5 draws on this evidence; the first full run with 1 draw is archived on the DGX in runs/harmonise/archive_run1. Use fit_transfer(..., depth=median_depth(query)) when the reference is deeper.

## Caveats (experiment agent)

1. Confidence labels. High: within-stage group-level harmonisation is reliable; LR beats kNN; cross-file label names do not line up; only CM transfers cleanly between E8.75 and E9.5. Medium-high: top-call is the best within-stage composition estimator; novelty does not catch missing types. Medium: the depth rules (about 2-point effects, one stage pair per direction); the KO-vs-WT shifts as differences between these two files. Low: the KO-vs-WT shifts as Mab21l2 genotype effects.
2. One embryo per genotype and no littermate WT in the KO file. The section bootstrap covers only within-embryo sampling of sections, not embryo or litter variation, and sections are a systematic rather than random sample. The KO and WT were sectioned differently (36 z levels at step 10 vs 40 at step 15, units unknown) and may cover different anatomy; lower NCC, endoderm and ectoderm in the KO could mean less head or pharynx was sectioned. The within-CM comparison is less exposed to this but not immune.
3. A classifier reads expression changes within a cell type as composition changes. If Mab21l2 loss shifts SV-CM cells toward an atrial or ventricular profile, they would look like SV-CM depletion; the cross-tab with the KO's own labels cannot separate the two.
4. 'Accuracy' means agreement with the organisers' annotation, which is itself a clustering; most errors fall between neighbouring labels (early-VEC/Chamber-Endo/BEC, aPHM/Lateral-PAM/pPHM, DMP). Cross-stage 'truth' at group level comes from configs/lineage_map.yaml, so part of the cross-stage disagreement is lineage-map placement (e.g. CardiacProgenitor means JCF/SHF at E8.75 but DMP at E9.5) rather than classifier error.
5. E8.75's 1,396 'Unknown' cells are excluded from training and evaluation. Cross-stage estimator tests use an oracle restriction to cells whose group exists in the reference. The z-half contrast test includes regional differences within cell types, but not genotype or depth shifts. All splits use one seed (0); fold SDs are reported, and the 20% hold-out matches 5-fold CV to within 0.005.
6. Two design changes were driven by results inside this run and are reported, not hidden. The default number of depth-thinning draws went from 1 to 5 after the first full run (archived on the DGX as runs/harmonise/archive_run1). The novelty residual component was added after the kNN-only score failed a synthetic novel-type test. All final numbers come from one invocation of the final code (manifest e8ad724d3a1d71bb / b89ae001f4b7ccf3); I diffed every CSV against the earlier copies and found them identical apart from timing columns.
7. The local count of 38 passing tests includes other agents' new tests. Files under experiments/coords and experiments/density, and the DGX tmux session vec-density-review, belong to other agents and were not touched. All data processing ran on the DGX; locally I ran only ruff, pytest and markdown rendering of small aggregate CSVs. No h5ad or expression matrices left the DGX, and nothing was committed.

## Independent review (2026-10-19)

An independent agent re-ran the analysis on the DGX with its own code. Its verdict, unedited:

All of the harmonise experiment's numbers reproduce. Its label-transfer and depth conclusions hold, but two KO conclusions need correcting before they go into docs/PLAN.md.

**What holds**
- An independent reimplementation on the DGX matches the reference hold-out, KO composition and within-CM figures to within 0.001-0.008. Seeds 0-2 confirm the cross-stage and depth results.
- The KO calls are stable across LR and kNN, depth matching or not, and a per-gene batch correction.
- I found no code bug that changes a result, no leakage between truth and prediction cells, and no data-rule violation.

**What needs correcting**
1. **KO-vs-WT group composition is not a genotype effect.** This is one WT embryo against one KO embryo, and the two z-stacks cover different anatomy. The WT stack's top quintile is mostly head/pharyngeal tissue (7% CM, 22% NCC); the KO's top quintile is still 31% CM. The section bootstrap is internally sound but only covers within-embryo section sampling. Trimming the stack ends shrinks NCC from -0.043 to -0.014, removes Ectoderm, flips CM, makes Pericardium non-significant, and flips EpicardiumST's sign. Only CardiacProgenitor down, Endoderm down and NCC down (size 0.014-0.051) survive every trim.
2. **'Top call is the best estimator' only holds when the classifier has seen the query.** When trained on one z-half and applied to the other (the analogue of WT-trained calls on the KO), top call underestimates label-level changes by about a quarter (slope 0.76), while EM's slope is about 1.04.

**Most robust KO finding:** the within-CM shift (V-CM 0.39 to 0.61, SV-CM 0.16 to 0.04). It survives a CM-only classifier, the batch correction and all trims, and agrees with the KO file's own labels. It is still n=1 per genotype, and the two embryos were sectioned at different planes.

**Smaller issues:** EM intervals used 1,000 draws, not 2,000; a few statements are overstated (LR vs kNN, 0.3 points, 'same picture'); section_ids is fragile on E8.75's x.5 z values. The required REPORT.md for this experiment does not exist.

**Recommendations for docs/PLAN.md**
- Adopt vec.harmonise as the label-transfer utility (high confidence): logistic regression, summed group probabilities, fit_transfer(depth=median_depth(query)) when the reference is deeper, 5 thinning draws for deeper queries.
- Do not put the Mab21l2 group-level differences into configs/t3_scenarios.yaml as effect sizes (high confidence). If anything, use the within-CM proportions and the trim range.
- When the Gata4 replicates arrive, measure rep1 vs rep2 to estimate how much composition varies between embryos of the same genotype, and compare z-coverage profiles before comparing any two files.
- Quote top call and EM together as a range for composition from other files (medium confidence).
- Treat cross-stage group accuracy as agreement with the hand-built lineage map (it is not validated yet), and revise the KO DPM/LPM, Branch Arch and CardiacProgenitor entries.
- There is a gene-level technical shift between the KO and WT files (rank correlation 0.32-0.74 across unrelated matched cell types). It does not affect the calls, but T3's systematic component must model it.

### Code issues found by the review

1. No bug that changes a reported number. An independent reimplementation of the classifier, OOF, thinning and bootstrap reproduces the hold-out, KO composition and within-CM figures to within 0.001-0.008.
2. Design issue in stage_contrast (run_harmonise.py, around line 621): it scores estimators with the hold-out model, which was trained on cells from both halves. That makes it an in-distribution test, so it cannot support the estimator choice for out-of-distribution files like the KO. A shifted-query version reverses the conclusion: top-call slope is about 0.76 at label level vs about 1.04 for EM.
3. Interpretation risk in stage_ko / bootstrap_difference: 'ci_excludes_0' only reflects section-resampling noise within each embryo (and the bootstrap is well calibrated for that). It carries no between-embryo or sectioning-coverage variance, so with n=1 embryo per genotype it should not be read or labelled as a KO effect.
4. section_ids (harmonise.py:132) is fragile. All 35 E8.75 z levels sit exactly on x.5 values, and integer rounding (half to even) works only because each section stores a single exact float; any noise around x.5 would split sections. Rounding to the stage's z step, or clustering with a tolerance, would be safer.
5. run_harmonise.py:304 sets n_boot_em = min(n_boot, 1000), so the EM intervals use 1,000 draws, while the report says 2,000 draws per embryo.
6. _novelty (harmonise.py:269) does not exclude self-matches for query cells. predict() on the model's own reference cells therefore gives biased-low novelty. This is an API caveat; it does not affect the reported results.
7. Reporting nits: 'LR beats kNN on every metric' is false for assigned accuracy (kNN is higher at lower coverage), and the kNN log-loss is inflated by hard zeros. 'Within 0.003' is 0.0036. 'OOF within 0.3 points of the annotation' holds only at group level (label level up to 0.48). 'Mean probabilities give the same picture' is not exact (Ectoderm is not significant with soft weights). fig_ko_crosstab.png shows thresholded fractions while the text quotes top-call fractions.
8. Data quirk to note: the KO stack has one 30-unit z gap (2 missing levels) next to a 4,170-cell section (about 3x the median, the only section with two sample_ids). These are probably merged sections and act as one bootstrap cluster.

## Lead's decisions (2026-10-19)

- **Adopted:** `vec.harmonise` is the label-transfer utility: logistic regression on 50 PCs, group probabilities summed
  from fine labels, Unassigned below 0.5, a deeper reference thinned to the query depth, 5 thinning draws for a deeper
  query.
- **Cross-file compositions only through `vec.harmonise`,** quoted as a top-call/EM bracket. The review showed that top
  call alone understates label-level changes by about a quarter when the query is out of distribution. Top call alone
  is used only within a file.
- **Knockout vs wild type:** the group-level differences are file-against-file (the stacks cover different anatomy) and
  are not used as genotype effects or T3 magnitudes. The within-CM shift is the robust summary. On release day, the
  Gata4 replicate difference sets the noise bar (`ASSUMPTIONS.md` A19).
- **Lineage map:** the KO's LPM maps to Pericardium and its DPM to PharyngealMesoderm (`file_overrides`); a merged level
  is used for cross-stage work, and CardiacProgenitor and Pericardium stay out of cross-stage trends.
- **Applied by the follow-up run** (see "Revisions" below once added): section ids from `vec.spatial.snap_z`, EM draws,
  a shifted-query estimator stage, z-trim outputs as standard, duplicated records dropped, release-day stages.
