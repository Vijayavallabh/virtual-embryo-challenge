What the read cannot show (written before the run):
  - The oracle shift is fitted on the truth's own library (its other half): in-sample by construction. It carries that library's own signature (dissection, capture, ambient RNA), which no developmental direction could predict, so the room A measures can be non-developmental.
  - Both families' sizes are chosen with the truth (the best of five multiples of each): the arms are optimistic bounds, never an estimate of what a step rule would deliver. The best of five sizes is not the best of every size, and one common multiple for all types is not each type's best: when the best projection is the smallest or the largest multiple a passing test reads 'not settled', and when the best oracle is the smallest or the largest a failing test reads 'not settled'; a size between two tried multiples is not covered.
