### A. A second direction in T1's chemistry from Leonard's nuclei: counted, then pooled with Feng's by ruling

**Facet:** expression. **Groups:** direction (de_direction .25; +3.01 / 9.49 left) and DE recovery (de_score .25;

**Mechanism.**
- The factor is computed per chamber category, in N2's form: log f = t x [log(m_Leonard + 0.5) - log(m_base + 0.5)].

- The factor is applied in count space by `vec.core.apply_factors` with its keep-depth constant. The 23 held-flat genes

- Leonard's WT nuclei are called into our categories by a classifier trained on the base stage's own labels only.
- At test, a rule fixed in the same row pools Feng's and Leonard's step-scaled log factors per gene and type: an
  equal-weight mean, or one source where only one covers the category. A gene the two sources sign oppositely shrinks
  toward zero.

  on others. Pooling studies brings it to 2.65 weeks [task-a 6].
- Genes whose change agrees across contexts can be forecast (MASE 0.78); the others do not beat carry-forward
  [task-a 5].
- Protocol differences outweigh within-protocol contrasts. Pseudobulk correlation is 0.84-0.86 between protocols and
  0.90-0.95 between tissues within one protocol [task-b 17]. So the partner should share T1's protocol, as Leonard does
  and JAX (sci-RNA-seq3) and mdCLA (whole cells) do not [task-b 3, 5, 20].

  a direction.
