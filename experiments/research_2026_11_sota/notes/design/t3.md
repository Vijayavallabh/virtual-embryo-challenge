**The two counted folds cannot see the part of s1's factor that is certain at test.** For any knockout scored against
WT E8.75, dt contains -u_ref, the reference file's own run and embryo deviation. s1's factor, measured against the same
file, carries it too. This is the "WT half transfers for certain" of PLAN 4.4, and it is real credit under a
shared-reference scorer [task-c 1][task-c 2]. Neither counted fold has it:

| Fold | Source factor | Target and reference | Shared reference? | A factor carried without it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| T3/Gata4->Mab21l2 | Gata4 against WT E8.75 | Mab21l2 against WT E9.5 | no | K2a 50.08 against our WT copy 50.74 |
| T3/Mab21l2->Gata4rep2 | Mab21l2 against WT E9.5 | Gata4 rep2 against WT E8.75 | no | the Mab21l2 factor against its own WT scored 52.04 / 46.24 on the Gata4 replicates, against 52.39 / 50.61 for WT cells (T3 script) |

So on both folds, shrinking a transferred factor toward identity gains points. At test the opposite holds. On the
replicate fold, which shares the reference, W1 put the exponent 1.0 above 0.5 and 0.75: 73.51 against 72.25 and 73.35
with depth on, means over the two replicates.

A fold gain from shrinkage is therefore explained by a known feature of the fold (TRIES rule 2, third condition). Every
factor try below carries a control arm, in K2b's logic: it moves as many genes toward identity by the same amounts,
chosen at random within deciles of WT E8.75 expression. The try keeps only a margin over both the base and the control.

A second constraint: a change written on s1's Gata4 factor runs on one fold only. K2a's record says "change does not
apply" on the other fold. So each candidate is written as an estimator applied to whichever knockout is the source.

### C3. A section-artifact audit and a section-robust source factor (one counted try)

  - Why no known feature explains a pass: the control absorbs the folds' preference for shrinkage. And on the

### C4. A lineage-distance source factor, free of extrinsic response and spillover (one counted try)

- **Facet and groups.** Expression. Response identity and direction, as in C3.
- **Mechanism and basis.**
  - What the literature says. Cell-extrinsic effects made up more than half of all effects across 50 zebrafish
    mutants [task-c 11]. Unperturbed neighbours respond to perturbed cells [task-c 15]. Segmentation adds neighbour-type
    molecules to a cell [task-c 18].
  - Why it matters. A source knockout's non-Mesp1 factor carries that knockout's own extrinsic response and spillover
    near the lineage. That is biology of the wrong gene for transfer, while run processing and the reference's own
    deviation are spatially uniform.
  - The change. Measure the factor only in non-lineage cells farther than a fixed in-plane distance from any
    Mesp1-lineage cell in the same or an adjacent section. For example 100 µm, fixed in the row before any run.
- **New against the pruned list and the 2026-11-05 report.**
  - It repeats the pruned "T3: a background- or spillover-aware factor" (+0.3 expected, SD about 1.5).
  - New: a fixed distance in place of a background threshold, and the shrinkage-matched control, which makes its sign
    checkable on the folds without tuning.
  - Still needed, as in the prune: a harness candidate, and the captain's ruling on rules note 5.
- **Legality.** As C3. Only the set of cells that measures the factor changes, not the cells drawn, so the composition
  is untouched.
- **Counted evidence.** Both folds, with C3's three arms and keep rule.
- **Expected gain.**
  - Mean +0.03 to +0.05 Overall, with a range of -0.5 to +1.5 T3. P(adoption) is about 0.1.
  - Upside: none.
- **Effort and risk.**
  - Effort: 2-3 days on the DGX CPUs.
  - Risk: fewer cells make a noisier factor. The size of extrinsic effects in non-lineage cells at E8.75 is unknown
    (task-c, Gaps).
  - C3 and C4 compete for T3's tries. Run C3's audit first; any extra part needs its own held-out gain (rule 7).
- **First step.** A target-free size check: in each source knockout file and in WT E8.75, the share of non-lineage
  cells beyond the distance, per label. Go on only if at least 30% remain in every label that enters the factor.
