1. Leonard et al. (2025) profiled plug-dated E14.5 C57BL/6 whole hearts with 10x Multiome nuclei (Rev F kit, CellRanger-ARC 2.0.2, CellBender ambient removal, ~10,000 nuclei targeted per library) in four genotype groups including WT, keeping 12,507 nuclei whose pooled shares are about 29% cardiomyocytes, 31% endocardial/endothelial, 26% fibroblasts, 9% epicardial and 3% immune, with no erythroid category named (GEO GSE272154; CC BY-NC-ND 4.0) [20]. [spot-check 1 — corrected (detail under Deep Read Notes [20]): every fact in this finding is in the source except "whole hearts", which the Methods do not state ("Hearts were dissected from embryos")]
2. mdCLA (2026) is a 10x v3 whole-cell atlas of 2,230,900 cells from 351 C57BL/6 embryos at 37 time points, 11 of them legal late-fetal points half a day apart (E14.0 to E19.0) and 18 at or before E9.5, with every embryo imaged for staging and E14.0-E15.0 embryos dissected into head, trunk and visceral parts under ACK red-cell lysis (8 in-window points: remove before use) [3][4]. [spot-check 2 — corrected: E19.0 is the birth sample, so the 11 points are 10 pre-birth points (E14.0-E18.5) plus birth; chemistry, strain, totals, staging, ACK lysis and licence verified]
3. mdCLA detects about 4x more genes per cell than Qiu 2024's sci-RNA-seq3 nuclei but only about 1.2x more at pseudobulk level, and 78 of 89 harmonised organ labels (87.6%) show similar cell distributions in the two atlases [3].

13. By FACS of whole mouse hearts, fibroblasts are 14% of all heart cells at E18.5, 10% at P1, 14% at P5, 18% at P15 and 27% in adults (adult myocytes 56%, endothelial 7%, vascular smooth muscle 10%) [13], whereas adult immunohistochemistry and refined flow put endothelial cells at more than 60% and fibroblasts at less than 20% of non-myocytes [14].
14. Protocol moves cardiomyocyte shares by tens of points: flow cytometry of 8-12 pcw human fetal hearts gives 75-80% cardiomyocytes (MHC+ 75%, n = 21), 9% CD31+ and 10-15% fibroblasts plus smooth muscle against "nearly half" cardiomyocytes in human fetal scRNA-seq [15], and adult mouse snRNA-seq gives about 23% cardiomyocytes against about 9% in Tabula Muris scRNA-seq [16].
15. Cross-protocol transfer of contrasts is weaker than within-protocol biology: in matched normal/tumour lung (4 patients, 10x 3' v3.1) epithelial normal-vs-tumour DEGs were 321 in cells and 947 in nuclei, between-protocol DEGs (3,480 normal, 1,156 tumour) outnumbered them, and pseudobulk correlations were 0.84-0.86 between protocols against 0.90-0.95 between tissues within a protocol, though shared DEGs exceeded chance [17]; an iPSC-cardiomyocyte time course (days 0-15) gave 962 vs 553 genes per cell and 20.3% vs 18.3% cardiomyocyte clusters in Drop-seq vs DroNc-seq but reported no fold-change concordance [18].
