## Deep Read Notes
### Source [1]: Nature Methods comment (Cao, Lu, Qiu 2026)
Key data: 5-page Comment; three approach families (descriptive atlases, rule/physics ODE and reaction-diffusion models, deep-learning foundation models); flow matching "inspired by optimal transport" learns vector fields linking states over developmental time; Spateo vector fields measure curvature, divergence and torsion of tissue deformation; no quantitative method results.
Key insight: the organisers' named obstacles are spatial registration to a standard embryo, temporal coupling across time points, scale (coarse-graining) and validation; they propose annual challenges because no accepted assessment criteria exist.
Useful for: method-summary vocabulary (Generality Award, co-authorship); no points.

### Source [2]: NeurIPS competition proposal
Key data: skill = d_base/(d_base + d), undefined metric -> skill 0; Table 1 weights; T3 distribution on the mutant state; bootstrap CIs over embryos, regions, cell types, genes, paired bootstrap between leaders; planned baselines: pseudotime and OT-coupled flow-matching extrapolation (T1), OT alignment and integration (T2), shift transfer and conditional generative models (T3), "transparent reference points rather than competitive upper bounds".
Key insight: the training knockout was chosen because its phenotype is "most obvious" and the gene "very specific"; Gata4 "also very specific"; the test gene "more broadly expressed". Baseline developers include Siyu He (Squidiff) and Nic Fishman; Yifan Lu leads tasks, metrics and baselines.

### Source [24]: i-habib community repository and ensemble-lab results
Key data: blends tested on the organisers' mini samples, built from the target itself ("not hidden-board estimates"): population mixture, mean graft (one candidate's pseudobulk on another's cells), quantile graft (one candidate's per-gene values by the other's within-gene ranks), spatial transplant (copy coordinates via expression matching).
Key insight: a blend keeps whatever property it copies; mean grafts carry DE and direction, quantile grafts carry marginals.
Useful for: confirming our "edited mean on real cells" design; no counted evidence.

### Source [25]: P2 methods journal

Useful for: idea inventory only; nothing counts as evidence under our adoption rule.
