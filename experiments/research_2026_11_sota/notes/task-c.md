## Findings
1. In four CRISPRi Perturb-seq screens (K562, RPE1, HepG2, Jurkat), split-half-corrected response variance divides into a shared cell-line "template" 27.8%, a conserved perturbation part 29.4%, a cell-line-by-perturbation interaction 23.5% and noise 19.3%; the template is 23% (K562), 28% (Jurkat), 49% (HepG2) and 65% (RPE1) of response energy, and 92-98% of it lies in the top 100 control (non-targeting) PCs (first control PC alone 12-74%) [3]. [audit spot-check 2: verified]
2. In a 15-cell-line by 88-drug atlas, one additive term common to all drugs of a context carried a median 48% of response variance on the shallow-control plate; removing it from both prediction and truth cut the calibration-mean score from 0.590 to -0.021 and a ridge adaptation policy's mean gain from 0.355 to 0.0001 [1]. [audit spot-check 1: verified]
3. With 2, 3 or 5 measured responses in a new context, adding their mean to a shared predictor scored 0.388/0.340/0.419 against 0.463/0.447/0.463 for the control-free pooled predictor under independent controls; oracle-weighted adaptation gains rose only from 0.0128 (k = 2) to 0.0179 (k = 40), the best fixed rule was 0.3 shrinkage (0.0148 at k = 5), and a split-control release rule correlated with realised gain at Spearman 0.77 (shallow) and 0.88 (deep) [1]. [audit spot-check 1: verified]
4. Scoring against a shared control inflates methods that read that control 1.5-2.4x (mean +0.082 over 52 benchmark entries; 40 of 52 changed rank) and more at low control depth (+0.722 vs +0.224) [1]; under shared-control PearsonΔ a perturbed-mean baseline reached about 0.8 and two control-only groups exceeded 0.75, both near 0 with split controls, and top-20-gene PearsonΔ reached 0.87 on a simulated null [2]. [audit spot-check 1: verified for the [1] figures; the [2] figures were not checked]
5. Transfer to an unseen context recovers only the conserved part: zero-shot MLP alignment with the conserved component r = 0.25-0.39 (ridge 0.10-0.13), the interaction not recovered, three pooled source lines beating one (p < 1e-10); within a line the template is predicted at r = 0.62-0.75 but template-removed responses only at 0.20-0.26 [3]; with 30% of perturbations seen in the new context, State still lost to the context mean on genetic fold change by 12% [8], and transporting source-context responses (PerturbMap) cut MSE by 4.1% [9]. [audit spot-check 2: verified for the [3] figures]
6. A typical CRISPRi perturbation changes about 45 genes and an essential gene over 500, but FDR-significant genes hold only 13-36% of the transcriptome-wide impact; impact correlates 0.40-0.75 across cell lines (mean 0.62) while replicate raw log-fold-change vectors correlate only 0.16 (impact 0.90) [10].
7. Among 50 zebrafish transcription-factor mutants pooled at 24 hpf (226,492 cells, four replicate pools), the median mutant changed the abundance of one major cell type but the transcription (> 10 DEGs at FDR < 0.05, |log2FC| > 0.5) of 12 cell types, and cell-extrinsic effects (cell types that never express the gene) were more than half of all observed effects [11]. [audit spot-check 4: verified]
8. ZSCAPE (23 crispants, >= 8 embryos per condition against 26 stage-matched controls at 24 hpf) found about 1,470 DEGs per perturbation summed over cell types, cell-type DEGs not associated with abundance changes, and DEG counts tracking cells per embryo (r = 0.62), i.e. DE sets are power-limited [12].
9. In vivo, comparisons against a separate control sample are dominated by sample-level shifts: in mosaic Drosophila midgut, edited and unedited cells of perturbed animals showed highly correlated changes against control animals (batch and stress genes), while within-animal genotype contrasts were almost orthogonal to them [13]; mouse-liver Perturb-Multi compared each knockout with control sgRNAs from the same mouse and found 109 of 406 targeting vs 0 of 50 control sgRNAs changing transcriptional state (imaging: 84/406 vs 3/50) [14].
10. Mutant-embryo staging is done by giving each mutant cell the mean developmental time of its k nearest neighbours in a time-resolved WT reference, with the warning that litter mis-staging can masquerade as mutant delay [16]; somite-matched individual embryos, not uterus-mates, separate delay-driven DE from mutant-specific DE [17] (both cited for method only).
11. Across 641 MERSCOPE brain sections (8 instruments, about 2 years), detection differed about 2-fold between sections of one mouse, fell with depth in the section (median plane-6/plane-0 ratio 0.34; 0.79 at 14 um vs 0.42 at 10 um), varied about 30% across x-y, and 31% of sections had dropped images that removed groups of 60-104 genes sharing one readout probe [20]. [audit spot-check 3: verified]
12. MERFISH per-gene detection depends on where a barcode's "1" bits fall in the imaging rounds (later rounds dimmer with older protocols; this readout-position score explained much of the between-protocol per-gene variation in culture and part of it in tissue), blank-barcode rates repeat across replicate runs, and readout buffers aged 7 days open to air caused a "massive" count loss [19]; per-gene ratios between chemistries are not uniform (cross-chemistry r about 0.91; bulk concordance 0.7 vs 0.8) [21].
13. Segmentation admixture makes neighbour-type markers dominate within-type DE when neighbourhood composition differs (11 of 36 top up-genes of tumour- vs stroma-fibroblasts were malignant markers; about 25% and 30% of top factor results false) and a sNCV-NMF-CRF clean-up removed them [18]; gene-count-based normalisation on skewed targeted panels creates false DE that area or volume normalisation avoids [22], and library size is spatially confounded with tissue biology across 27 samples and 4 platforms [23].
14. Control-referenced Pearson and unweighted MSE have near-zero dynamic range for many perturbations (a technical duplicate lost to the mean baseline under MSE in about 95% of K562 genome-wide perturbations), whereas DEG-weighted MSE, weighted delta-R2 and rank metrics separate positive from negative controls [6]; control-referenced deltas reward mode collapse [7]; DEG overlap and DE-AUPRC show limited dynamic range and DEG counts track statistical power [5]; perturbation strength varies within a label and a shared high-minus-low pattern reproduces across perturbations (split-half cosine 0.789 and 0.952 vs 0.154 and 0.127 shuffled) [4].
15. Phenotype only (no genes): after Mesp1-Cre-driven loss of canonical Wnt signalling the cardiac crescent and linear heart tube form, but looping and right-ventricle formation fail, leaving a primitive (left) ventricle with a shortened outflow tract while atria and inflow form; [removed: W — stage-resolved cellular-level phenotype details taken from a timeline partly inside the heart-interpolation window], and no general growth retardation or gross defect of pharyngeal mesoderm, somites or limb buds is reported [26]; [removed: W — knockout phenotype description from a source whose mutant observations lie at stages inside the T1/heart window].

## Deep Read Notes
### Source [1]: split-control criterion for few-shot adaptation
Key data: shared additive term = median 48% of response variance (shallow plate); calibration mean below the control-free predictor at k = 2/3/5 (0.388/0.340/0.419 vs 0.463/0.447/0.463) [audit: in [1] these three figures are the shared predictor plus the calibration mean, as finding 3 says]; control-reading methods 1.5-2.4x higher under shared controls; carriage coefficient a = -<dP, dc>/||dc||^2 (0 for control-free predictors, 1 for the calibration mean at unit weight); in a sciPlex3 depth ladder the shared-control term fell 76% with control depth, the independent term 5%.
Key insight: a few-shot correction estimated against the same control used for scoring partly reproduces that control's error, so it looks good under shared-control scoring and loses under independent controls; release it only when its leave-one-out gain against an independent control half is positive.
Useful for: T3, where dp and dt share pb(WT E8.75): s1's Gata4 factor is a k = 1 "calibration mean" with a near 1 at test. Caveat: their carriage is cell-sampling error that shrinks with control depth; ours is mainly one WT embryo's run idiosyncrasy, which does not.
### Source [2]: spurious correlation from shared controls
Key data: PearsonΔ of a perturbed-mean baseline up to about 0.8, of two control-only groups > 0.75, both near 0 with split controls (B = 100 random splits); top-20-gene variant 0.87 on a simulated null; the Xu 2024 ranking of perturbed mean vs GEARS reverses.
Key insight: the bias is positive whenever true correlation < 1, falls with control cell number, and is larger when truth sits near the control (weak responses), which is T3's case.
Useful for: reading T3's de_direction and severity as metrics that credit any component shared with the WT reference; the split estimator is conservative (attenuates real signal).
### Source [3]: perturbation response decomposition (template, conserved, interaction, noise)
Key data: template 27.8%, conserved 29.4%, interaction 23.5%, noise 19.3%; template 23-65% of energy by line; 92-98% inside top-100 control PCs; template predicted at r 0.62-0.75, template-removed at 0.20-0.26; zero-shot conserved r 0.25-0.39 (MLP), interaction not recovered.
Key insight: what transfers across contexts is the shared template and a conserved component; context-specific interaction needs about 30% of perturbations measured in the target context; the template lives in the control's own principal axes.
Useful for: pricing what a 1-2-knockout estimator can carry (the template-like part only) and motivating projection of a transferred factor onto the WT's own principal (technical) axes (lever C).
### Source [13]: scPT-seq internal controls in vivo
Key data: all cell subsets of perturbed animals, edited or not, show highly correlated changes against separate control animals (batch and general stress genes); within-animal edited-vs-unedited signatures are almost entirely orthogonal to those.
Key insight: in vivo, the sample-level shift dominates a perturbed-vs-control comparison and is best measured in unperturbed cells of the same animal.
Useful for: independent support for s1's design (factor measured in non-Mesp1 compartments of the knockout file) and for treating that factor as mostly run/sample-level rather than biology.
### Source [20]: MerQuaCo (MERSCOPE QC over 641 sections)
Key data: about 2-fold section-to-section detection differences even within one mouse; median plane-6/plane-0 ratio 0.34 (thickness-dependent: 0.79 at 14 um, 0.42 at 10 um); about 30% x-y variation; 31% of sections with dropped images that remove gene groups sharing a readout probe (60-104 genes per readout); losing 40 species changed about 10% of cluster labels.
Key insight: run and section effects in MERFISH are multiplicative and gene-structured (readout groups, z), not a single depth scalar.
Useful for: a physical model of the per-gene run factor (depth + readout-group + z terms) and for target-free audits of each released file (levers A and C).
### Source [26]: Mesp1-Cre loss of canonical Wnt signalling (phenotype only)
Key data: crescent and linear heart tube form; looping and RV formation fail; primitive (left) ventricle and short outflow tract remain; atria and inflow form; [removed: W — stage-resolved cellular-level phenotype details taken from a timeline partly inside the heart-interpolation window]; no general growth retardation; pharyngeal mesoderm, somites and limb buds grossly normal.
Key insight: the loss is local to the SHF-derived RV/OFT programme [removed: W — stage attribution of the defect from the same timeline]; there is no whole-embryo developmental delay, and [removed: W — cellular-level inference drawn from the removed timeline details].
Useful for: T3 scenario risk only (composition is closed): it supports sparing FHF/LV states and argues against a global delay axis; it is weakest support for depleting pharyngeal-mesoderm progenitor states.

## SOTA-to-VEC mapping
1. Shared-reference carriage and the split-control release rule [1][2][5]
- Does: measures how much of a prediction's score comes from reusing the scoring control, and releases a few-shot correction only when its leave-one-out gain against an independent control half is positive.
- Evidence: 52 benchmark entries and 6 datasets; inflation 1.5-2.4x for control-reading methods; release rule vs realised gain Spearman 0.77-0.88; on the shallow plate it withheld all 120 harmful adaptations.
- Moves: T3, all four groups. Our scorer is itself a shared-reference metric (dp and dt both subtract pb(WT E8.75)), so carriage of the reference's own deviation is real credit at test, not an artefact we can remove. s1's Gata4 factor carries it with a near 1; neither counted fold can show this, because Mab21l2's truth uses WT E9.5.
- Legality: a diagnostic on released files; target-free if built from WT halves (by section); legal. A WT cell split measures only cell-sampling carriage; the run-level term of a single WT embryo does not shrink with cells.
- Licence: preprints; Nicol's code fork github.com/phillipnicol/systema (licence not checked).
- Counted backtest: none for the diagnostic; any change it motivates goes to both cross-knockout folds.
- Expected gain: no direct points; decision value for the s2 swap pricing (U3, p_anchor): unknown.
- Pruned repeat: no; it bears on D5/U3. New: published sizes of carriage and an estimator (a) the team can compute.
2. Template decomposition with split-half noise correction; TRADE [3][10][4]
- Does: splits responses into shared template, conserved, interaction and noise using cross-half inner products; TRADE estimates total impact net of estimation error.
- Evidence: see findings 1, 5, 6; held-out perturbations and held-out cell lines; ridge/MLP vs scGPT, MORPH and STATE.
- Moves: T3 pricing only: how much of each released knockout's dt is template (reference plus run) vs lineage biology, which sets severity-gate risk.
- Legality: it reads the knockout responses, i.e. the counted folds' truths, so it spends folds (counts as a try).
- Licence: preprint code and TRADE package (licences not checked).
- Counted backtest: both folds would be spent.
- Expected gain: unknown (decision value only).
- Pruned repeat: yes, "T3: three diagnostics on the cross-knockout folds" (they would spend both folds); new is only a published estimator.
3. Within-animal internal controls and cell-extrinsic effects [11][13][14][15]
- Does: compares edited with unedited cells of the same animal (scPT-seq), knockouts with control sgRNAs from the same mouse (Perturb-Multi), and classifies effects as intrinsic or extrinsic (MIC-Drop-seq).
- Evidence: findings 7 and 9; extrinsic effects > half of all observed effects; Perturb-FISH reports that unperturbed neighbours respond and that cell density changes perturbation effects.
- Moves: T3 expression. It endorses s1's non-Mesp1 factor as the standard way to isolate the sample-level shift, and warns that the factor also carries Gata4-specific extrinsic biology and spillover, which will not transfer to beta-catenin.
- Legality: knowledge only; legal.
- Licence: n/a.
- Counted backtest: n/a.
- Expected gain: 0 directly; it motivates lever D.
- Pruned repeat: n/a.
4. MERFISH run structure: MerQuaCo, readout position, chemistry [19][20][21]
- Does: per-file QC (transcript density against a reference set, z-uniformity p6/p0, x-y periodicity, dropped-image gene groups); readout-position score per barcode.
- Evidence: findings 11 and 12.
- Moves: T3 (factor and depth step) and, in principle, heart and embryo run shifts between stages. It explains why per-gene run factors are run-specific (the team's two factors correlate 0.15) and gives them a structure, readout groups plus z and depth, that a target-free audit can estimate within each released file.
- Legality: QC of released training and validation files is legal. It must never touch a test file (class A).
- Licence: MerQuaCo code (Allen Institute; licence not checked).
- Counted backtest: both cross-knockout folds for any factor change.
- Expected gain: through levers A and C.
- Pruned repeat: the embryo depth line is closed; T3 depth is already in s1. New: readout-group and z axes.
5. Segmentation admixture correction [18][14]
- Does: removes neighbour-derived molecules per cell type by NMF on subcellular neighbourhood vectors plus a CRF.
- Evidence: finding 13; Perturb-Multi also attributes residual target signal to neighbours under imperfect 3D segmentation.
- Moves: T3 expression, in two ways. (i) Gata4's non-Mesp1 factor contains admixture that follows Gata4's own composition change. (ii) In the truth, non-lineage neighbours of the depleted RV/OFT/SHF cells lose spillover from them, a direction that s1's cell removal does not create.
- Legality: an expression edit with no composition change; frozen T3 rules need the captain's ruling.
- Licence: not checked.
- Counted backtest: both folds.
- Expected gain: the team's own price for the related idea, about +0.3 T3 (SD about 1.5).
- Pruned repeat: yes, "T3: a background- or spillover-aware factor". New: a distance rule replaces the background threshold, plus spillover-consistent depletion (lever D).
6. Normalisation pitfalls [22][23]
- Does: shows false DE from count-based normalisation on skewed panels and the spatial confounding of library size.

SpaNorm's point fits the team's D2 reading that the knockout files' deficit is lower capture per area: depth differences partly carry tissue and region.
- Legality: legal.
- Licence: SpaNorm is a Bioconductor package (licence not checked).
- Counted backtest: n/a.
- Expected gain: 0.
- Pruned repeat: the depth line.
7. Metric-calibration analyses and the severity slope [5][6][7][24][25]
- Does: calibrates metrics with positive controls (technical or interpolated duplicates) and negative controls (mean or control); shows mode collapse; flags scale sensitivity.
- Evidence: finding 14. Coherence and magnitude couple at Spearman 0.84-0.98, so weak responses carry little direction [25]. The replicated-profile Mann-Whitney pitfall (inflation factor about 7.62 at 100 copies vs 100 controls; the no-information control mean passes 69.2% of tests) [24] does not touch VEC's prediction side, which ranks dp.
- Moves: T3 severity (derivation, not literature). The zero-intercept slope regresses dp on dt, so on truth DE genes slope = rho x ||dp|| / ||dt||. A calibrated (posterior-mean) prediction undershoots at slope about rho^2, and a norm-matched one sits at about rho. The R^2 gate and both rank metrics are scale-free (the team's gate identity), so only mutant state pays for overshoot.
- Legality: an expression magnitude change; frozen T3 rules need the captain's ruling.
- Licence: n/a.
